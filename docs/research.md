# Why this rebuild

Chosen 20 October 2026 as the first rebuild in the security guarding family.

- **The bill.** TrackTik (owned by Trackforce, alongside GuardTek and Silvertrac) publishes no price. Its pricing page says the quote is built around site count and the modules you run, starting with tours and reporting, with billing, payroll, business intelligence and visitor management as add-ons (https://www.trackforce.com/pricing/, checked 20 October 2026). Its main rivals, Belfry among them, also quote privately.
- **Data-shaped.** Clients, sites, officers, licences, shifts, checkpoints, incidents and invoices, and weekly rituals: fill the roster, chase licences, follow up incidents, invoice the hours.
- **The rules are real and checkable.** New Zealand's PSPPI Act 2010 (certificates of approval, temporary certificates, emergency appointments) and NSW's Security Industry Act 1997 s 38A (subcontracting) turn into checks on the roster before anyone starts.
- **What stays with the custom build.** The guard phone app (sign-on, scans, incident photos) and live patrol maps.
