# Why there is no front end

TrackTik is a database with a subscription. Underneath are ordinary records: clients, sites, officers, licences, shifts, checkpoints, incidents, invoices. What you pay for, site by site and module by module, is the layer on top that lets people who do not write queries get at them: screens, filters, dashboards, forms.

That layer was the whole product when talking to a database was hard. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, ask "who can cover Ben's shift tonight?", and it checks every officer against the licence, induction, rest and subcontracting rules and answers. Ask a question the dashboard never had a chart for and you still get an answer.

## What you gain

- **Answers to your own questions.** Margin per hour by site, licences that lapse before the last rostered shift, how long each client waits to hear about an incident.
- **No per-site bill.** Winning a new site does not raise your software cost.
- **Your records in your database.** Back them up, read them from anything, leave any time.
- **The rules your way.** Change a house rule with one command, add a field in plain language.

## What a screen gives you that this does not

- **A phone app for officers.** Officers cannot sign on, scan a checkpoint or file an incident with photos from their phone. A supervisor records these here, or Enterprise DNA builds your officers a phone screen on the same database.
- **A live map of mobile patrols and GPS.** Not here.
- **A drag-and-drop roster board.** The roster is something you ask for and change by asking.
- **A client portal.** Clients get reports and invoices as documents, not a login.
- **Offline use.** It runs where Claude Code runs.

## Who this fits

An owner-run guarding firm whose office does the rostering, licence tracking, incident follow-up and invoicing, and who would rather ask than click. If your officers need an app in their hand all shift, keep it, and put this behind it as the office's database, or have Enterprise DNA build the app on top.
